The Moorabbin Kangaroos have capped off the home-and-away season in the most dramatic fashion possible, downing **Narre South Saints by 3 points** at Moorabbin Reserve in Round 18 — a finish that will be talked about around the club rooms for years to come.

Trailing by three points as the clock ran down and the siren ready to sound, Moorabbin won a contest deep in attack, launching the ball forward one last time. What followed was a **massive contested mark right in front of goal** — the kind that stops a crowd dead — with no time left on the clock.

With everything on the line, the mark was converted into a goal, and Moorabbin snatched the win by 3 points in scenes of pure pandemonium at Moorabbin Reserve.
